Five directions for this tool

Each one has a light and a dark version that follows your device. Pick one to try it: it applies to the tool pages for this tab. Round 1 of design review, awaiting the reviewer's notes.

Recommendation, round 1: Ledger. A repository tool is mostly lists, and Ledger shows the most at once while keeping contrast and focus rings. High contrast is the fallback for anyone who needs it. This will change after review.

Paper

Calm and warm, close to the other Sovereign Website pages. Roomy, sans-serif, green accent.

Reads like a document. Less dense, so long repository lists scroll further.

Terminal

Monospace throughout, square corners, green on near black in dark mode.

Suits people who live in a shell. Monospace body text tires some readers.

Ledger

Compact, small radius, blue accent, tight rows like a ledger.

Fits the most repositories and files on screen. Least breathing space.
