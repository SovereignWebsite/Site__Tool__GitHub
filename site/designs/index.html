Five directions for this tool

Each has a light and a dark version that follows your device. The demo link opens the real tool on invented data, so you can judge each one working. High contrast is not a direction: it is a button in the header that works on top of any of them.

Recommendation, round 3: Two-pane, using Ledger's tables in its left pane. It is the only direction that changes the layout of working in a repository; the reviewer prefers it to Ledger. Ledger stays as the single-column choice.

Paper

Calm and warm, close to the other Sovereign Website pages. Roomy, sans-serif, green accent.

Reads like a document. Less dense, so long lists scroll further.

Terminal

Monospace and square, driven from the keyboard: / filters the list, j and k move, Enter opens.

Fast for people who live in a shell. Monospace body text tires some readers.

Ledger

Real tables in one wide column: name and size, or number, title, author and date. Compact, blue accent.

Fits the most rows on screen and is easy to scan. Wide tables need a sideways scroll on a phone.

Cards

Repositories, files and issues as a grid of cards. Serif text, amber accent, generous spacing.

Friendliest to read and browse. Shows the fewest items at once.

  • example-org/example-repo can write

Two-pane

A structural change: a table on the left and the open file or issue on the right, filling the screen height, in teal. One pane at a time on a phone.

Fastest for moving between items. Needs a wide screen to pay off.
