Five directions for this tool

Each has a light and a dark version that follows your device. The demo link opens the real tool on invented data, so you can judge each one working. High contrast is not a direction: it is a button in the header that works on top of any of them.

Recommendation, round 2: Ledger, because a repository tool is mostly lists. Two-pane is the structural alternative if wide screens matter most. Both stay open until the reviewer has seen round 2.

Paper

Calm and warm, close to the other Sovereign Website pages. Roomy, sans-serif, green accent.

Reads like a document. Less dense, so long lists scroll further.

Terminal

Monospace throughout, square corners, green on near black in dark mode.

Ledger

Compact, small radius, blue accent, tight rows like a ledger.

Fits the most repositories and files on screen. Least breathing space.

Cards

Serif text, big rounded panels, amber accent, generous spacing.

Two-pane

A structural change: the list stays on the left and the open file or issue on the right, in teal. One pane at a time on a phone.

Fastest for moving between items. Needs a wide screen to pay off.
